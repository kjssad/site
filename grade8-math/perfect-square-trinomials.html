DISCOVER

What do you notice?

  1. The first and last terms are perfect squares.
  2. The middle term is 2 × √first × √last — look at the last column!
  3. The sign in the bracket is the sign of the middle term: (3x − 2)² gives −12x.
THE RULE

Perfect Square Trinomial (PST)

a² + 2ab + b² = (a + b)²

a² − 2ab + b² = (a − b)²

Read it backwards to factor: find a and b, then copy the sign of the middle term.

THE CHECK

Is it a PST? Three quick questions

  1. Is the first term a perfect square?
  2. Is the last term a perfect square?
  3. Is the middle term exactly 2 × √first × √last?

All three ✓ → write (√first ± √last)², with the sign of the middle term.

GAME

Error hunter

Sam wrote (x + 5)² = x² + 25. What is the correct expansion?

2 × x × 5 = 10x — the middle term is always there.

GAME · CHALLENGE ★

One more value of k

x² + kx + 49 is a PST when k = 14. Which other value of k also works?

(x − 7)² = x² − 14x + 49, so k = −14 works too.

HOTS

Expected reasoning

Check three things. First term 4x² = (2x)² ✓. Last term 25 = 5² ✓. Middle term 2 × 2x × 5 = 20x ✓. All three work, so it is (2x + 5)². If any check fails, it is not a PST and we need a different method.
