Factoring Quadratic Trinomials

For trinomials where the coefficient of x² is 1.

x²first termits coefficient is 1
+ 7xmiddle termits number is 7
+ 12third termpositive or negative

You will learn three rules, watch them work step by step, build them with algebra tiles, and then take a 10-question practice. At the end you will see whether you are ready and what to revise.

Rule 1

The third term is positive (+)

  1. List the factor pairs of the third term.
  2. Pick the pair whose sum equals the number in the middle term (ignore its sign for now).
  3. Both factors take the sign of the middle term.
Example: x² − 7x + 12
3 + 4 = 7, both negative
(x − 3)(x − 4)
Rule 2

The third term is negative (−)

  1. List the factor pairs of the third term.
  2. Pick the pair whose difference (bigger − smaller) equals the number in the middle term (ignore its sign for now).
  3. The factors have opposite signs. The bigger factor takes the sign of the middle term; the smaller factor takes the opposite sign.
Example: x² + 2x − 15
5 − 3 = 2, bigger is 5
so +5 and −3
(x + 5)(x − 3)
Rule 3

Check your answer

Expand your factors. If you get the original trinomial, your answer is correct. If not, recheck the signs or the factor pair.

(x + 5)(x − 3)
= x² − 3x + 5x − 15
= x² + 2x − 15 ✓

Limits of these rules

  • They work only when the coefficient of x² is 1. Other values come later.
  • No middle term (like x² − 9): use the DOTS (Difference of Two Squares) method instead.
  • Trinomials with no factor pair that works (not factorable) are not covered here. Every practice item is factorable.
